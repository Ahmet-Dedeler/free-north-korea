| **`northkorea.kr`** | **BLOCKED BY KISA** | Reserved/restricted country-name string under KISA rules. |

**Why `.kr` should only be a redirect, not the main domain:**
1. **Global SEO:** Google geo-targets `.kr` exclusively to South Korea, which hurts rankings for global English searches like *"how can North Korea be freed"*.
2. **Jurisdiction:** `.kr` domains fall under KISA/South Korean telecom rules (where North Korea-related links and materials face stricter administrative filtering). Keep the primary canonical domain on `.org`.
